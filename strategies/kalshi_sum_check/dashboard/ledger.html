Paper only. No order is ever sent. Fills are taken at the walked book prices after a re-check delay, every leg or none, and paper fills are netted from the local books so the same resting orders can't be filled twice. Real fills would compete with other traders, so treat these results as an upper bound.

Eligibility. Events whose outcomes the classifier marks EXHAUSTIVE or UNKNOWN. Unknown events are treated as capable for this test. A settled trade that loses money halts new trades.
