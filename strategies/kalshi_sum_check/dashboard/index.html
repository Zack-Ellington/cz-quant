How the numbers are made. Each scan walks every leg's order book to the target size in contract sets. Σask is the cost of buying YES on every leg, and Σbid is the proceeds of selling YES on every leg. The estimated return adds up taker edge for every event with a positive edge in the latest scan. It is shown before fees and after Kalshi's taker fee, which is charged on every leg as 0.07 × the series fee multiplier × contracts × P × (1 − P), rounded up to the cent. Only events still positive after fees count, and all ROI and APR figures in the table are after fees. APR is weighted by capital and runs to the event's expected settlement.

Read before trading. Mutually exclusive doesn't mean one outcome must win. Events where the mid prices sum below 0.90 are marked likely non-exhaustive and left out of the totals. Two-party races can also resolve with no listed winner.
